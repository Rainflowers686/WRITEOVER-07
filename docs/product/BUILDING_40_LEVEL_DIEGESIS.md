# BUILDING 40-LEVEL DIEGESIS — WRITEOVER-07 v1.2

## 1. Diegetic Vertical Structure

The building fully exists in narrative and simulation reference:

- B4
- B3
- B2
- B1
- 1F–36F
- Roof

Total diegetic levels: 4 basements + 36 floors + roof = 41 levels in fiction.

## 2. Playable Scope

v1.2 does **not** build 41 complete levels.

Targets:

- 12–14 playable regions
- 6–7 signature gameplay spaces
- ≈10 secret spaces

## 3. How Non-Playable Floors Are Expressed

The rest of the building should be felt through:

- Elevator
- Directory
- Radio
- Logs
- Windows
- NPC dialogue/routine
- Lockdown
- world state references

## 4. Topology Requirements

Full topology includes:

- Elevator
- Stairs
- Maintenance shaft
- External maintenance path

These connect the playable regions and make the 41-level fiction credible.

## 5. Signature Gameplay Spaces

The 6–7 signature spaces are chosen from systemic pillars, not decorative
rooms:

- security checkpoint / badge validation
- medical bay / injury economy
- maintenance corridor / repair + infrastructure
- research floor / evidence + secret truth
- a body-disposal-adjacent service area
- a terminal/command space
- a narrative or rooftop exit space

## 6. Secret Spaces

- EASTER_EGG: small, does not affect ending
- CACHE_SECRET: resource or side path
- DEEP_SECRET: may affect Truth, SelfKnowledge, Meta Ending

Ordinary easter eggs cannot all affect endings.
