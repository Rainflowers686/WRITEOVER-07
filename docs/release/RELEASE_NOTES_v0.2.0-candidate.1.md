# WRITEOVER-07 0.2.0-candidate.1

## 中文

这是供试玩的候选版本，不是正式 1.0。游戏仍是以字符绘制世界的第一人称探索与战斗游戏，保留现有 19 个房间、三种结局和 B1 至屋顶的流程。

这次更新主要解决游玩时容易卡住或误解的地方：

- 首次启动可选择简体中文或英文，游戏中也能切换。目标、对话、门禁反馈、档案与菜单都使用配对文字资源。
- 中文按显示列排版。终端尺寸变化时画面随之调整；窗口过小时暂停游玩，放大后恢复。
- 按键可以在暂停菜单中重新绑定。冲突会提示，修改需要确认。
- 设置增加了实际生效的视野、分项音量、提示强调和鼠标纵轴反转。战斗难度调整玩家承受的伤害，不改变通行路线。
- B1 提供简短的操作引导；案件档案显示已取得的关键记录。受击反馈会在来源明确时说明大致方位。
- 存档校验更严格，失败的替换不会先删除旧存档。最终决策前的恢复点与结局存档分开。

内容包含枪械、致命与非致命战斗、昏迷者和死者、藏匿身体，以及封闭机构中的悬疑情节。

实际终端字体、窗口操作、音频听感与首次试玩仍需要人工验收。自动回放和渲染图不能替代这些检查。旧候选包与本次包分开保留；升级前请备份自己的存档。

## English

This candidate is for playtesting, not a stable 1.0 release. WRITEOVER-07 still builds its first-person world from character cells. The existing 19-room campaign, three endings and route from B1 to the roof are unchanged.

The update focuses on making the game easier to read, control and recover:

- Choose English or Simplified Chinese on first launch, or change languages in Settings. Paired resources cover objectives, dialogue, access feedback, case files and menus.
- Chinese text uses display-column layout. The game adapts to terminal resizing and pauses when the window becomes too small to play.
- Rebind keys from Pause. Conflicting assignments are rejected, and changes require confirmation.
- Field of view, separate audio volumes, interaction emphasis and inverted mouse Y have live controls. Difficulty changes incoming player damage, not access routes.
- Brief contextual hints guide the B1 opening. Case File now shows records you actually acquired. Damage feedback can identify a coarse direction when the source is known.
- Save loading rejects malformed state more strictly. Failed replacement preserves the previous file, and the pre-final recovery point stays separate from the completed ending.

Content includes firearms, lethal and non-lethal combat, unconscious and dead bodies, hiding bodies, and institutional suspense.

Terminal fonts, foreground window behavior, audio and first-time play still need human acceptance. Automated replays and rendered captures do not establish those results. Older candidates remain separate; back up your saves before upgrading.
