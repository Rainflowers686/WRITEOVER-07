# WRITEOVER-07 — Chapter One manual-play checklist

Status for every row starts as `PENDING_MANUAL`. Rain should fill the receipt
template after a first-time foreground session. Do not convert a row to PASS
from replay logs, pipe output, source inspection, or the package smoke alone.

Use a clean extracted classroom package when possible. Start it from an
unrelated working directory in a real foreground Windows Terminal. Record the
terminal size, package/version, start/end time, and every confusing moment.
Use a fresh user-data directory for each route comparison.

| ID | What to do | What should happen | Failure looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Launch | Start the packaged entry from outside the repository. | The banner appears, authored character/text data loads, and saves/settings go to user data outside the package. | Missing-data error, fallback art/text, hang, crash, or package writes into its read-only content root. |
| B. Terminal / visual | In foreground Windows Terminal, use a sensible size (at least 80x30), resize once, and inspect the full frame. | Text, character art, HUD, objective and prompts remain readable without clipping; input remains usable after resize. | ANSI/TrueColor fallback is mistaken for acceptance, clipped columns/rows, stale frame, broken restore, or unreadable overlap. |
| C. Controls | Press F1; try movement/look, interact, fire, reload, weapon slots, pause/quit, save/load. Also try sprint/jump/crouch/prone/lean/melee if listed by the build. | Help matches the build. WASD/mouse/F/LMB/R/1-3/Esc/F5/F9 work; RMB is visibly reserved because ADS is disabled. | A documented control does nothing, an essential control is undiscoverable, or ADS is advertised as active. |
| D. Quiet route | Wake in B1, move, search the downed guard, use the stunner/non-lethal route, drag and hide the body in the cart, use the badge/terminal/reader, and continue through Calibration. | The first actionable objective updates; body and cart prompts track state; the quiet route reaches Medical and the staff passage without developer intervention. | The hint disappears before movement/interaction, a prompt asks for an impossible action, precise pixel aim is required, or the route soft-locks. |
| E. Aggressive route | From fresh user data, take the badge and use the pistol/loud route against the authored Guard, then continue through the reader and Security. | Hit/death/access feedback is clear; the aggressive route reaches its authored Security/checkpoint state and differs from the quiet route. | The Guard cannot be hit for a normal reason, death has no readable feedback, the route is identical to quiet, or the player cannot recover. |
| F. Camera consequence | Compare an online-camera loud action with disabling the B1 camera before the same kind of action. | Online observation produces a noticeable later security response; offline produces a noticeable blind-spot route. The player sees contextual feedback, not raw flags. | Both runs behave identically, the camera state is not understandable, or a debug identifier is shown. |
| G. Cleaner / history | Establish the positive Cleaner interaction, hide/discover the body, then save/load and revisit the relevant route. | The later Cleaner response reflects the earlier relationship/body choice and remains coherent after load. | Cleaner response is always neutral, history disappears, body disposition resets unexpectedly, or the player cannot infer why the response changed. |
| H. Medical | Enter Medical, face the intake terminal, use it, and read the resulting objective. | Intake is a real interaction; the room explains the next choice between the authored staff/security paths. | Medical is functionally empty, the terminal prompt lies, or a valid credential is rejected without recovery information. |
| I. Staff | Take the quiet staff passage, inspect the route clue, and use the service door toward the elevator lobby. | The shift-change clue and staff route are mechanically real; the service door offers a meaningful quieter access path. | Staff is only alternate text, the clue has no effect, or the service door is a dead end. |
| J. Security | Try the authored stealth/badge, stunner/non-lethal, and pistol/aggressive possibilities. Break LOS behind geometry and return. | The player can tell when the Guard notices, attacks, is hit/stunned/killed, loses LOS, and stops attacking; a valid route continues. | Damage occurs through a wall, the Guard attacks from another room, a prompt targets the wrong actor, or combat feedback is ambiguous. |
| K. Save / load | Save quiet and aggressive mid-route; also save after the camera event, Cleaner history, body handling, recent NPC activity, and low health where naturally reachable. Load, move, and save again. | Room, facts, quest/objective, body, camera, NPC/relationship, checkpoint, health/ammo and access remain semantically coherent. Transient motion may resume according to the bounded runtime contract. | A route fact, body, camera, health, checkpoint, or relationship disappears/mutates, or save/load requires a developer reset. |
| L. Death / restart | Die before a checkpoint and press F9; separately die after a valid checkpoint and load it. | `YOU ARE DOWN` and the F9 recovery path are clear. No-save death restarts the room; checkpoint death returns to the valid checkpoint state. | Death is silent, stale movement/fire/objective/subtitles survive incorrectly, or the player is trapped without a recoverable path. |
| M. Elevator / completion | Reach the elevator lobby through quiet and aggressive/authorized routes; focus the restricted door and interact. Reload the completed state once. | Incomplete routes are denied with a useful explanation. An authorized run records the Chapter One checkpoint/completion once and remains coherent after reload. | The elevator accepts a skipped route, completion is not visible, completion repeats every frame, or reload reopens/rewrites the chapter state. |
| N. Quit | Press Esc, resume once, then pause and press Q. | The game exits normally, restores the terminal, and does not leave a hung process or damaged console. | Q is undocumented/ignored, the process hangs, or the terminal remains in a broken mode. |
| O. First-time comprehension | Give the build to a person who has not read replay fixtures or source; observe the first route without coaching. | At each major room the player can say what they saw, what they know, what to try next, and how to recover after a mistake. | Progress depends on exact coordinates/frame numbers, hidden flags, developer instructions, or repeated unexplained waiting. |

After the session, copy observations into
`CHAPTER01_MANUAL_PLAY_RECEIPT_TEMPLATE.md`. Keep visual/art notes separate
from functional failures; final beauty, prose and audio remain outside this
functional readiness gate.
