## Protected direction

The world remains CharCell/Unicode Character-Art. Authored silhouettes, negative
space and structural glyphs must survive removal of color. No image rasterization,
Braille/half-block framebuffer, public renderer API or save-schema redesign.
Security retains helmet/visor/armour mass. Humans use restrained skin planes and
eyelines, not punctuation faces. Four directions remain separate authored poses.
Pistol is an oblique slide/grip/hand; Stunner has paired contacts and an emitter
body. Do not make them the same silhouette with different colors.

Narrator: a records supervisor, emotionally restrained, dry rather than jokey;
knows the facility's records, not the player's private motives. Obedience produces
an accepted account, improvisation leaves a different account, violence leaves
evidence rather than a morality score. Keep silence between consequential lines.
Security is procedural; Cleaner practical/social; Technician distracted/technical;
Medical clinical but human. Upper staff explain their actual role in the route.
