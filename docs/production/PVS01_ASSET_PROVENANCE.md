# PVS-01 GOLD Asset Provenance

The PVS-01 Gold closure intentionally uses small deterministic assets that are
owned by this repository. No external asset pack, unclear license, runtime LLM
or untracked middleware dependency is part of the production path.

## Visual assets

- World materials are generated by the production renderer in
  `src/render/production_renderer.cpp` from fixed material/color policy and
  Height-Span depth. The Half-block output is `240x67` terminal cells over a
  `240x134` logical-pixel buffer.
- NPC, terminal, lamp, door, medical, crate, elevator, camera and sign sprites
  are deterministic procedural silhouettes with depth-aware occlusion. Their
  current purpose is readable PVS production presentation, not a claim of a
  finished hand-authored atlas.
- The weapon viewmodel is deterministic renderer geometry anchored to the
  declared safe area. Recoil, muzzle flash, hit edge, explosion/debris and
  bounded screen-shake state are generated from the simulation/presentation
  state; no random external media is loaded.

## Narrator typography

The narrator intrusion uses an embedded deterministic 5x7 block glyph atlas in
`src/app/composition_root.cpp`. Large glyphs are composed diagonally across the
frame with staged reveal, accent/glitch treatment, bounded shake and world
visibility policy. Reduced flicker and reduced camera shake change the effect
intensity while retaining the complete text information.

## Audio

- Windows: `src/platform/windows/win_audio.cpp` synthesizes short bounded PCM
  cues in memory and submits them through the native WinMM `PlaySoundA` API.
  The backend identifies itself as `winmm-procedural` when a voice device is
  available and `winmm-no-device` when it is not.
- POSIX: `src/platform/posix/posix_audio.cpp` is an honest
  `posix-subtitles-only` presentation fallback. It does not pretend to have
  voice playback and keeps subtitle/narrative information available.
- The PVS uses short event cues for weapon fire, impact, door changes and NPC
  speech. The backend is presentation-only; semantic events remain in the
  EventBus and SaveManager paths.

## Data and runtime provenance

- NPC authoring begins in `data/npcs/npcs.json`, is validated by
  `tools/contentc/contentc.py`, and is compiled to the bounded binary
  `data/npcs/npcs.bin`. Runtime loads the binary and merges its spawn/health/
  perception data with identity/cognition/faction/role from the systemic seed.
- Systemic seed authoring begins in `data/systemic/systemic_seed.json` and is
  compiled to `data/systemic/systemic_seed.bin` by the checked-in content
  compiler.
- Replay scripts under `tools/replay/` are deterministic input fixtures used
  for evidence; they are not gameplay assets.

## Scope statement

These assets close the current Gold PVS presentation and evidence gates. A
future formal art/audio production pass may replace the procedural visuals or
WinMM cues behind the existing contracts, but that is a separate scoped task
and is not silently included here.
