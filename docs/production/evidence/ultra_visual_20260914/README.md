Critique/freeze: Roof now reads as an exterior pause after the institutional
interiors. Skyline is deliberately restrained, not a cinematic city asset.
Bodies have weight, fronts and backs differ, eyes are restrained. Door framing
and material seams stay world-anchored; stylized depth/edge stepping remains
visible up close. Room layouts retain grouped equipment and paths. This is fit
to freeze for mechanical audit, not a claim of photorealism or Visual Gold.

Raw large SVGs and scratch user-data stay locally reproducible; compact PNGs and
selected text receipts are committed. No historic evidence was deleted.
