Verdict vocabulary is deliberately narrow:

* CONFIRMED means the finding was real and the bounded current-scope
  correction is present and exercised.
* ALREADY_FIXED means the finding is not present at the current head and was
  fixed before the remediation commit or is already covered by an existing
  invariant.
